Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Are the start and end dates included?

Yes. Both dates are included in the calendar range. Weekend and holiday exclusions are then applied.

What counts as a weekend?

This calculator treats Saturday and Sunday as weekend days.

Are all local holidays included?

No. Holiday support focuses on common nationwide public holidays. Regional, state, provincial and employer-specific holidays may differ.

Can I include weekends?

Yes. Turn off “Exclude weekends” before calculating.

Can I calculate without holidays?

Yes. Turn off “Exclude holidays” or choose “No holiday calendar.”
